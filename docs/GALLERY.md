# Einhander visual gallery

A look at the recompiled game, the facade and title experiments, and the complete equipment and finish concept collections. **Gameplay captures, reference artwork, and concepts are labelled separately.** Click an image to inspect its full-size PNG. Pixel content is preserved throughout; the two large game captures use lossless PNG compression for faster loading.

## City captures

### Recompiled stock textures, 4× rendering

The isolated stock session disabled HD textures and texture dumping. This is a 1280×960 capture of the recompiled renderer using stock textures, with 4× internal rendering and bilinear filtering. It is not an original-console screenshot.

## Provenance and scope

[Public image provenance](media/gallery/provenance.json) records each output's hash, byte size, measured dimensions, status, and sanitized source identifier. All gallery PNGs preserve the original pixel content and have no embedded text metadata. Concepts and references remain byte-identical copies; the two 2880×2160 game captures are losslessly re-encoded for compression, with source and public-file hashes recorded separately. Original design identities belong to their respective rights holders; this is independent fan recompilation and remaster work.

The gallery contains no ROM, BIOS, replacement pack, extracted source-word archive, model weights, private machine paths, or generation prompts. Resolution labels describe the supplied images; no full-game completion or 4K/60 FPS claim is made.
